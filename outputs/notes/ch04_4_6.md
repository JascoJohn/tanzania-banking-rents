# Chapter 4.6: risk economics

Generated by `tzbank.chapters.ch04.risk` (stage `profitability`). Blueprint §4, 4.6; hypothesis H4.2. Business-model
level; chapter 8 tests the credit cycle and capital efficiency in depth.

## Research question

How much of each bank's lending margin survives credit losses, and how efficiently does each bank use risk-weighted
capital?

## Variable definitions

Cost of risk = impairment / average gross loans (§2); NPL ratio = stage-3 gross loans / gross loans (IAS 39 impaired
loans before FY2018); coverage = loss allowance / stage-3 loans; NIM = NII / average IEA; risk-adjusted NIM = NIM -
cost of risk; return on RWA = net profit / average RWA (Group for CRDB from FY2015, Bank-solo for NMB).

## Sources and transformation trail

`data/processed/bank_year_wide.csv`; function `risk`. Table `outputs/tables/ch04_4_6_risk.csv`.

## Validation test

`tests/test_ch04_03b.py`: impairment / average assets equals the 4.0 tree term; hand case for risk-adjusted NIM.

## Results (Derived)

- CRDB FY2025: cost of risk 1.26% (2015-18 mean 3.50%), NPL ratio 2.7%, coverage 66%, risk-adjusted NIM 7.54%, return on RWA 5.57%.
- NMB FY2025: cost of risk 0.84% (2015-18 mean 2.67%), NPL ratio 2.4%, coverage 102%, risk-adjusted NIM 8.18%, return on RWA 7.46%.

## Evidence class

Tables: **Derived**. Statements under *Draft interpretation* are **Interpretation**.

## Limitations

- IAS 39 to IFRS 9 in FY2018 changes both the NPL definition (stage 3) and impairment timing (blueprint L4).
- RWA bases differ (NMB Bank-solo; CRDB Bank-solo to FY2014, Group from FY2015): the same capital-basis RWA
  as chapters 7 and 8; return on RWA compares levels only roughly.
- One credit cycle (2015-18) in the sample; chapter 8 handles through-the-cycle cost of risk.

## Interpretation

**Interpretation.** Impairment explains much of CRDB's 2015-18 trough and its recovery (4.0); NMB's higher NIM leaves a wider buffer after credit costs. Whether today's low cost of risk is durable is a chapter 8 question.

## Cross-cutting explanations of rent (blueprint §0)

A low current cost of risk against the 2015-18 levels bears on **temporary credit-cycle conditions**: part of the
recent ROE may be cyclical (the 4.0 recovery attribution gives impairment the largest share of CRDB's recovery).
