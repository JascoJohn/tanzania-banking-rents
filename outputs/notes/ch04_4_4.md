# Chapter 4.4: transaction economics

Generated by `tzbank.chapters.ch04.transactions` (stage `profitability`). Blueprint §4, 4.4; hypothesis H4.2.

## Research question

How much does each bank earn from transactions (fees by type, FX), per customer, agent and unit of assets, and how
is the payments market around the banks moving?

## Variable definitions

Fee income = gross fee and commission income (note total); fee types as in 4.1; FX income = net foreign
exchange income (income-statement line); fee per customer / per agent use the KPIs (MEDIUM confidence,
shown with a +/-10% band); market context = NPS panel and Table H12 series, **market-level**.

## Results (Derived)

- CRDB FY2025: fees 3.07% of average assets; digital channels 45.7% and cards 12.5% of fees (LOW confidence: label-matched fee types); FX 0.49% of average assets.
  Fee per customer FY2021: TZS 63,253 (band 57,502-70,281; MEDIUM).
- NMB FY2025: fees 4.02% of average assets; digital channels 24.5% and cards 13.3% of fees (LOW confidence: label-matched fee types); FX 0.68% of average assets.
  Fee per customer FY2024: TZS 122,290 (band 111,173-135,878; MEDIUM).
- Market-level: mobile money transaction value grew 18.0% a year 2019-2025 (NPS, all providers).

## Evidence class

## Limitations

- KPIs (customers, agents) come from narrative pages, are sparse and MEDIUM confidence; per-KPI ratios are bands.
- Fee categories are mapped from printed labels (4.1): fee-type shares, including the digital share, are **LOW**
  confidence and are excluded from every 4.7 verdict until the fee-type mapping is verified.
- NPS series are market totals: they describe the market the banks operate in, not each bank's share.

## Interpretation

**Interpretation.** Transaction income is a secondary engine for both banks (4.0 ranks non-interest income behind leverage and asset yield in the gap). Fast market-level growth of mobile money is a disruption risk to bank transaction fees and to cheap transactional deposits.

## Cross-cutting explanations of rent (blueprint §0)

Fee depth from cards, agents and digital channels bears on **distribution and market access** and **customer
switching costs**; rapid mobile-money growth at market level is evidence of **digital disruption** risk to fee and
deposit franchises (a vulnerability for 4.7), not of a bank-level advantage.
