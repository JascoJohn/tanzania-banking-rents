# Chapter 12: USD returns and implementation

Generated by `tzbank.chapters.ch12_usd_implementation` (stage `usd`). Blueprint §4 chapter 12; H12.1, H12.2
(proposed). Expected TZS returns are chapter 9's (register v1.3); repository private; publication blocked by open
issue 19 (CMSA licensing). **No investment advice; the market-access table is desk research for counsel review and
draws no legal conclusions.**

## Research question

What did NMB and CRDB return in USD, what would a USD investor expect after depreciation and trading costs against a
USD hurdle, and can a large fund build and exit a position on the DSE?

## Variable definitions

TZS total return = (P_t + D_t) / P_t-1 - 1 (year-end prices; D_t = dividend paid in year t); USD return = (1 + TZS
return) x FX_start / FX_end - 1 (TZS per USD, end-December); E[r_USD] = (1 + E[r_TZS] - round-trip cost) / (1 + d) - 1;
break-even depreciation d* = (1 + E[r_TZS] - cost) / (1 + h) - 1; hurdle h = US 10-year + EM premium; days to build =
position / (participation x ADV); cost per side = DSE fees (tiered brokerage + VAT + market fees) + Amihud impact
(ILLIQ x daily trade value). Definitions in `docs/VARIABLE_DICTIONARY.md`.

### Hypotheses (Interpretation)

- **H12.1: INCONCLUSIVE.** CRDB HIST: E[r_USD] -18.3% vs hurdle 12.2% (TZS -15.4%, cost 5.88%, depreciation 3.5%; break-even depreciation -24.6%); NMB HIST: E[r_USD] 46.0% vs hurdle 12.2% (TZS 51.1%, cost 5.22%, depreciation 3.5%; break-even depreciation 35.5%); CRDB CURR: E[r_USD] -28.5% vs hurdle 12.7% (TZS -25.8%, cost 5.84%, depreciation 3.8%; break-even depreciation -34.4%); NMB CURR: E[r_USD] -16.1% vs hurdle 12.7% (TZS -12.9%, cost 6.74%, depreciation 3.8%; break-even depreciation -22.9%). Rule: base case (USD 20m at 20% of ADV, 'history' depreciation): supported when E[r_USD] after round-trip cost exceeds the USD hurdle for both banks at both dates; rejected when it falls short in every case; inconclusive otherwise.
- **H12.2: INCONCLUSIVE.** CRDB HIST: 596 days to build USD 50m at 20% of ADV (binds); E[r_USD] -18.3% vs hurdle 12.2% (valuation binds); NMB HIST: 6787 days to build USD 50m at 20% of ADV (binds); E[r_USD] 46.0% vs hurdle 12.2% (valuation does not bind); CRDB CURR: 302 days to build USD 50m at 20% of ADV (binds); E[r_USD] -28.5% vs hurdle 12.7% (valuation binds); NMB CURR: 985 days to build USD 50m at 20% of ADV (binds); E[r_USD] -16.1% vs hurdle 12.7% (valuation binds). Rule: per bank and date, capacity binds when building USD 50m at 20% of ADV takes more than 20 trading days; valuation binds when the base E[r_USD] does not exceed the hurdle. Supported when capacity binds and valuation does not everywhere; rejected when capacity does not bind everywhere; inconclusive otherwise.

## Evidence class

Historical returns and FX: **Derived** from Observed data; costs and impact: **Estimated**; depreciation and expected

**Interpretation**.

## Limitations

- End-December TZS/USD for 2023 and 2024 is not held (the 2024/25 BoT report prints fiscal-year columns), so 2023-24 are
  translated only as part of the Dec 2022 - Dec 2025 span; collection task.
- Forward US inflation is TO COLLECT; the PPP scenario uses the FOMC 2% goal as a stated assumption.
- The daily data start on 2025-08-21: the 31 Dec 2025 capacity uses about four months of trading; Amihud impact is a linear
  average and understates the cost of trades much larger than the typical day.
- The fee schedule is desk research pending the owner's copy. The USD hurdle uses the TZS COE's beta,
  ERP, CRP and LP (issue 33 resolved); the US 10-year replaces the TZS long-bond yield net of the default spread.
- Dividend withholding tax for a foreign holder (`ch12.div_wht_foreign`, 5% for DSE-listed companies per the desk
  research) is deducted from the expected dividend yield; treaty rates and the tax position of the holder are not
  modelled (counsel review).

## Interpretation

**Interpretation.** Read with chapters 9 and 11: the USD case rests on the TZS expected return, which the base-centred Monte Carlo puts below the price for both banks at 31 Oct 2026; depreciation and a round trip of several percent widen the gap. Capacity is a separate constraint: a large position takes weeks to months to build at a fifth of daily traded value.

## Cross-cutting explanations of rent (blueprint §0)

Chapter 12 does not test the rent explanations; it asks whether a USD investor can capture the value chapters 9-11
estimate. Thin trading and high transaction costs are themselves a barrier that limits arbitrage of any mispricing.
