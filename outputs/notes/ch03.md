## Research question

Does systemic importance give NMB and CRDB a funding advantage, is it offset by higher capital requirements, and do
their valuation multiples carry a premium for it?

## Variable definitions

Headroom = capital ratio - requirement (BoT minimum + conservation buffer; NMB and CRDB as in chapter 7); deposit-cost
advantage = smaller banks' median cost of deposits - NMB / CRDB mean; P/B = market capitalisation / attributable equity
(year-end); systemic-bank premium = coefficient c in P/B = a + b ROE + c SIB.

### Hypotheses (Interpretation)

- **H3.1: INCONCLUSIVE.** cost of deposits: smaller banks' median exceeds the NMB / CRDB mean by 4.03% on average (11 years); stated Tier 1 requirement: systemic banks 11.72% vs others 11.90% on average (no higher requirement before NMB's December 2025 D-SIB designation, surcharge not stated); held headroom: systemic 6.8%, others 24.8%. Rule: supported when the deposit-cost advantage exceeds 100 bp AND systemic banks face a higher stated requirement; rejected when there is no advantage; inconclusive otherwise.
- **H3.2: NOT REJECTED (LOW POWER).** pooled P/B = 1.14 + -0.76 ROE + c SIB: c = +0.32 [-0.06, +0.71] (90%), n = 61 bank-years, 7 banks; profitable bank-years only (ROE > 0): c = +0.34 [-0.01, +0.69], n = 49; East African peers TO COLLECT. Rule: H3.2 (no systemic premium) is not rejected when the 90% interval of the systemic-bank coefficient includes zero (low power: 56 bank-years, 7 banks, a wide interval), supported when it lies below zero, rejected when it lies above zero; the ROE > 0 specification is reported as a robustness check.

## Limitations

- The BoT's announcement and list of designated banks are not held; NMB's designation is known from its own report
 . CRDB's status is unknown.
- Smaller banks' capital ratios are as reported (Bank = Group); their requirement is the BoT minimum plus the buffer.
- DCB, MKCB and MUCOBA report ratios below the requirement in some years (as published; no adjustment).
- The P/B panel is small and unbalanced (listing dates, thin trading); peers are not held.

## Interpretation

**Interpretation.** NMB and CRDB fund far more cheaply than the smaller listed banks, while the stated capital requirement was the same for all banks until NMB's December 2025 designation; any offset so far is NMB's own choice to hold more capital, not a requirement.

## Cross-cutting explanations of rent (blueprint §0)

A deposit-cost advantage without a matching capital requirement is consistent with a **regulatory advantage** or a
perceived too-big-to-fail **funding subsidy** as well as with **low-cost deposits** from the franchise; chapter 3
cannot separate them. Chapter 6 measures the franchise net of costs.
